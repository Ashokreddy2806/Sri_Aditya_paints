Helpful before you buy

Common questions, clearly answered.

How do I order a specific shade?

Use the official Berger or JSW catalogue, note the shade name or code, and send it with your preferred product and pack size. We will confirm availability before you pay.

Can you tint paint while I wait?

Yes. We offer instant tinting for eligible shades and products in 1 kg to 20 kg packs. The team will confirm the correct base and availability first.

Can I order online?

Yes. Send a quote request on WhatsApp through this site. We will confirm the exact dealer price, product and collection options with you.

Which finish should I choose?

Bring a photo of the room or book a consultation. We can help you choose the right preparation, product and finish for your surface and use case.
